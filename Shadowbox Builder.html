Slots are cut to the shelf thickness plus the clearance. Measure your sheet with calipers; 1/8" ply is often 3.0 mm, not 3.175.

Cubbies

Rows run top to bottom. Set how many cubbies each row has; they start out equal. Height is relative, so 2 is twice as tall as 1. To fine-tune, drag shelves and dividers on the Front view.
